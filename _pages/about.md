I am a PhD researcher in the Kreitman School at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev.

My work sits at the intersection of empirical narrative analysis, LLM-based annotation, and the statistical validation of narrative frameworks applied to literature and movie synopses.

My research develops and evaluates methods that treat narrative structure as something measurable: testing computational and LLM-based judgments against expert gold standards across literary and filmic corpora.

I am currently working on several projects in computational narratology, spanning automatic literary evaluation, narrative-stage analysis, and the reliability of LLM-based annotation.
